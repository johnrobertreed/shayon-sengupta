# Shayon Sengupta

**Shayon Sengupta** is an American investor. He is a general partner and co-head of venture at [Multicoin Capital](https://multicoin.capital/). He joined the firm in 2020, became an investment partner in March 2023, and was named general partner and co-head of venture in March 2026. His work covers public-market thesis and coverage plus private-market deal sourcing for the firm's funds.

## Education

His personal site states that he studied mathematics at the University of Chicago.

## Career

In March 2026 Sengupta was named general partner and co-head of venture at Multicoin Capital, alongside Spencer Applebaum. Managing partner Tushar Jain wrote that the two would continue to help drive thesis formation and portfolio strategy across funds, and described them as among the primary authors, with him, of the firm's updated 2026 investment thesis.

In March 2023 the firm named him an investment partner, again with Applebaum.

He joined Multicoin in 2020. His personal site says he helped grow the firm's assets under management into the billions.

Previously he worked at Abodewell, a quantitative market maker in residential real estate, and on the Strategy and Business Development team at SAP.

## Work

Sengupta works across public and private markets. In public markets he focuses on thesis formation, asset coverage, and on-chain diligence. In private markets he identifies, sources, and leads deals across decentralized physical infrastructure networks (DePIN), compute, and on-chain infrastructure, and helps drive portfolio strategy across the firm's funds.

His personal site describes him as a primary author, with Applebaum and Jain, of Multicoin's updated 2026 investment thesis. His writing covers on-chain market microstructure, internet labor markets, and decentralized physical infrastructure.
